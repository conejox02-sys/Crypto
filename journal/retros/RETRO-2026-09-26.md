# Retro 2026-09-26: v1 -> v2

## What the data said

Data: 8,640 KuCoin SOL-USDT 15m candles, 2026-06-28 to 2026-09-26 (price
$70.8 -> $121.6, +72%). Folds are three equal consecutive 30-day windows,
each warmed up on the prior data. Buy and hold per fold: +5.3%, +46.0%, +12.3%.

## Diagnosis

- v1's signals had a thin gross edge; ~0.26% round-trip cost (0.1% taker each
  side + slippage) turned it into a steady loss. Losing trades averaged -1.44R
  because costs were ~0.44R on a 1.5-ATR 15m stop.
- The pullback setup was worst (PF 0.28): buying RSI dips on 15m bars in a
  trend is noise at this cost level.
- Fixed 2.5-ATR targets cut winners short. Winners in v2 average +5.1R.

## Change (v2)

Breakout only, only when ATR >= 0.4% of price (the move must be big enough to
clear fees), 30-bar Donchian high on volume > 1.5x average, stop 1.5 ATR,
target 8 ATR, breakeven at +1R, hold up to 24h.

Why I trust it more than a single best config: all 5 configs that survived
every fold (out of 432) share this shape, and 90 of 96 neighbours are net
positive. Breakeven setting doesn't matter much (0, 1 or 2R all give similar
results).

## What could still be wrong

- Selection bias: v2 was chosen on the same 90 days. The live demo ledger is
  the real out-of-sample test. It trades ~3.5 times a week, so a verdict needs
  2-4 weeks, not one day.
- Regime: the sample is a bull run. The EMA200 filter keeps it flat in
  downtrends, but it has never been tested in one.
- 25% win rate: expect streaks of 5-8 losers (about -1.3% each at 1% risk).

## Proposals for the operator

- Timeframe lives in protected config. 1h tested worse than 15m here, so no
  change is needed now, but it belongs to the strategy.
