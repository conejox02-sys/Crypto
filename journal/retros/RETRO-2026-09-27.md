## Diagnosis

v2 was fitted to the Jul-Sep bull run (yesterday's retro flagged exactly this
risk). Its trend filter, EMA200 on 15m bars (~2 days), lets it buy every bounce
inside a bear market. Breakouts then fail and each costs ~1.3R.

## Honest check of the fix

A 30-day regime filter picked by looking at the whole year would be hindsight.
The check that matters is a rolling walk-forward: each month choose among 48
configs (regime off/10/20/30d, slope, min ATR, target) using only the prior
90 days (cash if nothing scored > 0), then trade the next 30 days.

- Walk-forward, 8 out-of-sample months (2026-01-25 to 2026-09-26):
  **+10.7%**, 41 trades. SOL over the same window -2.2%; fixed v2 -9.4%.
- It chose cash for Jan-Mar and the 20-30d regime filter every month after.

## What is still true

- In a falling market this strategy does not make money; it avoids losing it.
  Over the 8 bear-heavy months even v3 was -9.9%. Its profit comes from bull
  months. That is the ceiling of a long-only spot strategy; the alternative
  would be futures shorts, which is an operator decision (config allow_short).
- Sample: 68 trades a year (~1.3/week). Judging the demo will take weeks.
- January 2026 lost 6% in 13 trades: a bear-market rally lifted the 30-day EMA
  briefly. Watch for this pattern.
